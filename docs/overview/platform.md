# Platform (Web App)

Copy pageCopy page

Use Fish Audio in your browser, no code required

Copy pageCopy page

The [Fish Audio web app](https://fish.audio/app) gives you every capability without writing code: generate speech, clone voices, produce long-form projects, and manage your account. Sign in at [fish.audio/app](https://fish.audio/app).

This page is an orientation map of the web app. Use the links below to open each tool. To build with code instead, see the [SDK Quickstart](https://docs.fish.audio/developer-guide/sdk-guide/quickstart) or the [API Reference](https://docs.fish.audio/api-reference/introduction).

## 

​

Create audio

## Text-to-Speech

Type or paste text, pick a voice and model, and generate speech.

## Voice Changer

Upload audio and transform it into another voice.

## Speech-to-Text

Upload audio to transcribe it, with timestamps.

## Sound Effects

Generate cinematic sound effects from a text prompt.

## Music

Generate music from a description.

## Audio Separation

Split audio into stems (e.g. vocals and background).

## 

​

Voices

## Voice Cloning

Create a custom voice from your own audio samples.

## Professional Voice Clone

Clone a verified voice at studio quality, then release it. Step-by-step guide.

## Voice Design

Design an original voice from a text description. Step-by-step guide.

## Voice Library

Browse and use thousands of community and official voices.

## My Voices

Manage the voice models you’ve created or saved.

## Pronunciation Dictionaries

Create, preview, and publish reusable pronunciation rules. Follow the [dictionary guide](https://docs.fish.audio/developer-guide/core-features/fine-grained-control/pronunciation-dictionaries) to use a published version in TTS requests.
